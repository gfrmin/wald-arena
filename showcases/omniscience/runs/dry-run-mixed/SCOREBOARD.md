Generated by `showcases/omniscience/run.py`; do not edit by hand. Brief 002, revision 2 (wald charter-v0.2 / surface-v0.2 / kit-v0.13), measured as it is (rule 7).

**Dry run on stand-ins — not the frozen models.** The primary is `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, the second opinion `claude-sonnet-5` and the grader `claude-sonnet-4-6`: three models, so the instruments can disagree and the grader does not grade its own answers. The plates play from the recorded answers and grades and make no call. The numbers below prove the plumbing: that every observation is recorded and priced, every pack is lawful, ships its Counts and is played by wald on a plate, and every contestant is scored from the same record. They say nothing about the frozen models named in brief 002, nor about the pre-registered claims, which are untested until QUESTIONS.md's pre-registration is ruled.

- Run: `showcases/omniscience/runs/dry-run-mixed`. Questions: 294 (144 calibration, 150 test), 49 per domain, drawn with seed 1, split with seed 20260923; each plate takes the test questions in the order of seed 20260925.
- Instruments: primary `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` at $0.0007 declared per call; second `claude-sonnet-5` at $0.0015 declared per call; grader `claude-sonnet-4-6` at $0.0069 declared per call.
- Grader: `claude-sonnet-4-6` with AA's published prompt — **not AA's grader** (`gemini-2.5-flash-preview-09-2025`, no longer served; QUESTIONS.md 2.9). It is the After-act: it grades the answer each episode submitted, or the read on an abstention.
- Buckets b0, b1 (cuts [80]). Globals (2.15), uniform over 128 values: ρ_b ∈ {7/20, 13/20, 17/20, 19/20} per bucket; (a⁺, a⁻) ∈ {(4/5, 1/5), (1/2, 1/2)}; (σ, α, β) ∈ {(1/2, 4/5, 1/5), (1/10, 4/5, 1/2)}; γ ∈ {9/10, 1}.
- Penalties p: 1, 3, 10. Second-opinion prices c (utility, a right answer = 1): 1/10, 1. Agreement (5 samples) costs 5 × the primary's declared price × λ_usd = 1.
- *score*: mean utility of the answers (+1 right, −p wrong, 0 partial or abstained); ×100 at p = 1 is the Omniscience Index. *net*: score less what the contestant bought (grading, which every contestant gets, left out). Δ: paired per-question difference in net, ± 2 standard errors. *realised*: wald's mean utility less every price its episodes paid, the After-act's included, and c for each blind switch (E7).
