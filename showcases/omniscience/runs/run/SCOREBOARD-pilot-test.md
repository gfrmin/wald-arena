- Buckets b0, b1 (cuts [90]). Globals (2.15), uniform over 384 values: ρ_b ∈ {7/20, 13/20, 17/20, 19/20} per bucket; (a⁺, a⁻) ∈ {(4/5, 1/5), (1/2, 1/2)}; (σ, α, β) ∈ {(1/2, 4/5, 1/5), (1/10, 4/5, 1/2)}; γ ∈ {9/10, 1}; κ ∈ {0, 1/2, 9/10}, the instruments tied (2.25).

Δ: paired per-question difference in net, ± 2 standard errors. *realised*: wald's mean utility less every price its episodes paid, the After-act's included, and c for each blind switch (E7).
